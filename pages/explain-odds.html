Basic Concepts

A deck has 52 cards. You grab 5 cards. There are 2,598,960 different combinations of 5 you could grab. That's your "total."

Every hand is just: (how many of those 2,598,960 combinations look like MY hand) / 2,598,960.

To count "how many combinations," we ask: "How many ways can I pick X things from Y things?" We write that as C(Y, X).

Example: C(4,2) means "pick 2 suits from 4 suits." The answer is 6, because the pairs are: (hearts, spades) (hearts, diamonds) (hearts,clubs) (spades,diamonds) (spades,clubs) (diamonds,clubs).

That's all the math. Every hand below is just multiplying "pick X from Y" numbers.

Each Hand

ROYAL STRAIGHT FLUSH (10-J-Q-K-A, all one suit)

You get to pick the suit: 4 choices. The cards are fixed. No other choices.

Combinations = 4

Probability = 4 / 2,598,960

STRAIGHT FLUSH (five in a row, same suit, NOT a royal straight flush)

Pick where the run starts: 10 spots (2-6, ..., 10-A). Pick the suit: 4 choices. Subtract the 4 royals (already counted above).

Combinations = 10 * 4 - 4 = 36

Probability = 36 / 2,598,960

FOUR OF A KIND (four of one value + one other card)

Pick the rank for the four: 13 choices. All 4 suits are used: 1 way (no choice). Pick the 5th card's rank: 12 choices. Pick the 5th card's suit: 4 choices.

Combinations = 13 * 1 * 12 * 4 = 624

Probability = 624 / 2,598,960

FULL HOUSE (three of one value + two of one value)

Pick the rank for the triple: 13 choices. Pick 3 of its 4 suits: C(4,3) = 4 ways. Pick the rank for the pair: 12 choices. Pick 2 of its 4 suits: C(4,2) = 6 ways

Combinations = 13 * 4 * 12 * 6 = 3,744

Probability = 3,744 / 2,598,960

FLUSH (five same suit, NOT a straight flush)

Pick 5 ranks out of 13: C(13,5) = 1,287 ways. Pick the suit: 4 choices. Subtract the 40 straight flushes (10 runs x 4 suits).

Combinations = 1,287 * 4 - 40 = 5,108

Probability = 5,108 / 2,598,960

STRAIGHT (five in a row, mixed suits, NOT a straight flush)

Pick where the run starts: 10 spots. Each card can be any suit: 4 * 4 * 4 * 4 * 4 = 1,024. Subtract the 40 all-same-suit ones (straight flushes).

Combinations = 10 * 1,024 - 40 = 10,200

Probability = 10,200 / 2,598,960

THREE OF A KIND (three of one value + two other different cards)

Pick the rank for the triple: 13 choices. Pick 3 of its 4 suits: C(4,3) = 4 ways. Pick 2 other ranks (different from each other): C(12,2) = 66 ways. Each of those 2 cards picks a suit: 4 * 4 = 16 ways.

Combinations = 13 * 4 * 66 * 16 = 54,912

Probability = 54,912 / 2,598,960

TWO PAIR (two of one value + two of one value + one other card)

Pick the 2 ranks that will be pairs: C(13,2) = 78 ways. Pick 2 of 4 suits for the first pair: C(4,2) = 6. Pick 2 of 4 suits for the second pair: C(4,2) = 6. Pick the 5th card's rank: 11 choices. Pick the 5th card's suit: 4 choices.

Combinations = 78 * 6 * 6 * 11 * 4 = 123,552

Probability = 123,552 / 2,598,960

ONE PAIR (two of one value + three other different cards)

Pick the pair's rank: 13 choices. Pick 2 of its 4 suits: C(4,2) = 6 ways. Pick 3 other ranks: C(12,3) = 220 ways. Each of those 3 cards picks a suit: 4 * 4 * 4 = 64 ways.

Combinations = 13 * 6 * 220 * 64 = 1,098,240

Probability = 1,098,240 / 2,598,960

HIGH CARD (no pair, no straight, no flush)

Pick 5 ranks that don't make a straight: C(13,5) - 10 = 1,277. Assign suits so it's NOT a flush: 4^5 - 4 = 1,020.

Combinations = 1,277 * 1,020 = 1,302,540

Probability = 1,302,540 / 2,598,960
